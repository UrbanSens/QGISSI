about=
    QGISSI (QGIS Spatial Intelligence) is an AI agent that works on the project you have open.

    • Your model, your key: Anthropic Claude (Opus 5.5 by default), OpenAI, Google Gemini, any OpenAI-compatible server, or Ollama for fully local, offline use. No account, no subscription, no vendor server in between.
    • 50 tools: project and layer inspection, statistics and expressions, every QGIS Processing algorithm (search, help, run) with CRS-unit guards, styling and labels, OpenStreetMap and geocoding, editing, map rendering the model can look at, print layouts, and guarded PyQGIS code.
    • You stay in control: approval modes, a code safety guard with a runtime tripwire, untrusted-data labelling against prompt injection, secret masking, and one-click undo per run.
    • Open prompts and tools, live token and cost meter, no telemetry.
    • Benchmark harness: QGISSI-Bench (30 verifiable QGIS tasks) plus adapters for Google's GeoKTools and GeoExpertQueries, to compare models on accuracy, cost and latency.
